As easy as starting a video call.

A pier is a room for files. You open it, people join, and everyone in it can share.

  1. Create a pier

    One tap gives you a link. That link is your pier.

  2. Let people in

    Send the link to anyone. When they open it, you get a request. Tap Let in, or don't.

  3. Swap files

    Everyone on the pier can share files, and everyone can download them. Both ways, any device.

Nobody sees what you send. Not even us.

Every pier has its own secret key, and it lives in your link. Only the people you send it to have it. We don't.

The key never reaches us

It sits after the # in your pier link. Browsers never send that part to any server, ours included.

Encrypted twice

Every connection is encrypted, and every file is encrypted again with AES-256 before it leaves your device.

Tamper-proof

If anyone tries to step in between you and your friends, us included, the connection is refused.

Nothing stored, nothing named

Files go straight from your device to theirs. We never hold a copy, and we never even see file names.

Bad connection? It carries on.

If the network drops halfway through a big video, your download picks up exactly where it stopped. Even if you refresh. Even if they do. You never pay for the same data twice.

Questions

Do my friends need an app or an account?
No. They open your link in whatever browser they have, type their name, and wait for you to let them in.
Is it really free?
Yes. No premium tier, no ads, no sign-up. PierHop is open source.
Who can see my files?
Only the people you let onto your pier. Not us, not your internet provider, nobody in between. You can remove anyone, and the pier disappears when you end it.
What can PierHop itself see?
Only what it takes to connect people: the pier code, the names people type when they ask to join, and network addresses. Never your files, their names, or your pier's key.
Someone sent me a pier link. What now?
Open it, type your name and wait a moment. Once they let you in, you'll see their files and can share your own.
What if my connection drops or I refresh?
You're back on the pier straight away, without knocking again, and downloads carry on from where they stopped.
What happens if I close the tab?
If you made the pier, it ends and the files you shared stop being available. Nothing stays behind anywhere.
Does it work on mobile data?
Yes. Wi-Fi, mobile data, home or abroad: it works wherever you can open a web page.
